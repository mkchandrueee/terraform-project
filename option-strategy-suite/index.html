    Execution guide

    The 13-step standard operating procedure this suite implements, phase by phase.

    Phase 1 · ATM selection & option data setup

    1. Identify the ATM strike. Open the NIFTY chart, note the open of the first 5-minute candle (09:15–09:20) and round it to the nearest 100. Open 23670 → 23600 CE and 23600 PE.
    2. Record option OHLC. In your broker app, note the open, high, low and close of that same 09:15–09:20 candle for both the ATM Call and the ATM Put.

    Phase 2 · Option Analyser (levels & confirmation)

    1. Enter values. Put the CE OHLC into the Call section and the PE OHLC into the Put section, then press Analyse.
    2. Read the output. Three levels are produced for each side: entry price, Target 1 and stop loss.
    3. Wait for confirmation. Never enter on touch. Wait for a 5-minute candle to close above the entry price — entry 150 means a close of 150.05 or higher confirms it.

    Phase 3 · T1 Decision Helper (target validation)

    1. Select the triggered side — Call target or Put target.
    2. Map the levels. Entry field ← analyser stop loss · T1 field ← analyser entry price · T2 field ← analyser Target 1.
    3. Enter the confirmation candle — the OHLC of the exact candle that closed above the entry price.
    4. Decide. Hold T2 means the probability of reaching target is high and the trade can be held. Book now / partial book means probability is low — exit or avoid.
    5. Check condition 4. Passed → proceed with the trade. Not passed → skip the trade, whatever the other checks say.

    Phase 4 · Stoploss Pullback Entry (liquidity setup)

    1. Enter the original first-candle data for both Call and Put.
    2. Check direction. The tool returns Call Buy or Put Buy — trade only the recommended side.
    3. Execute. Zone 1 is the entry price, Target 1 is the target. No stop loss is used with this tool.
